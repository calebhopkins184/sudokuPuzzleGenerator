Dad jokes work because they’re safe, short and shared. They’re clean enough for any audience, quick enough to slip into everyday conversation, and so obviously corny that the groan becomes an inside joke between the teller and everyone else. Learn the techniques in our pun guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dad joke?

A dad joke is a short, clean joke built on a pun or obvious wordplay, told with pride and usually met with a groan. The groan is part of the fun.

What is the best dad joke of all time?

It is hotly debated, but “Why couldn’t the bicycle stand up by itself? It was two tired” and “Hi Hungry, I’m Dad” are perennial favorites.

Are these jokes appropriate for kids?

Yes. Every joke on Dad GIFs is family-friendly, and we have a dedicated page of dad jokes for kids with knock-knock jokes and riddles.

Can I use these jokes in a speech or card?

Go for it. Classic dad jokes are folk humor shared by countless families. Use them in toasts, cards, lunchbox notes and group chats.

Does Dad GIFs host GIFs from movies and TV?

No. To respect copyright, we only use original drawings and CSS animation. Our GIF culture guide explains how to share and make GIFs responsibly.

When is Father&rsquo;s Day?

In the US, UK and Canada it is the third Sunday in June. Dates vary elsewhere, such as the first Sunday in September in Australia and New Zealand.
