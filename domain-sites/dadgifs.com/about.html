About Dad GIFs

Dad GIFs is a family-friendly humor site dedicated to the noble art of the dad joke.

What you’ll find here

  • Big, categorized collections of clean dad jokes
  • Jokes chosen for kids, from knock-knocks to riddles
  • A practical guide to writing and delivering your own puns
  • Brand-free Father’s Day gift ideas
  • An explainer on dad memes and GIF culture

Our house rules

  • Clean, always. Every joke is suitable for the dinner table.
  • Kind, always. We laugh at words, not at people.
  • Original artwork only. We don’t host copyrighted GIFs or images. Our animations are simple, original drawings and CSS.
  • No fake reviews or sponsored picks. Gift ideas are general categories, not product endorsements.

Where the jokes come from

Classic dad jokes are folk humor — passed around families, classrooms and offices for generations, with no single author. We collect and tidy the classics and write new ones in the same spirit.
