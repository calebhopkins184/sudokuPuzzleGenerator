The Best Dad Jokes, Sorted by Category

A dad joke is a short, clean, pun-based joke so corny that the groan is the punchline. Below are more than 60 of the best, sorted by topic so you can find the right one for the dinner table, the office or the car. All family-friendly, all reusable, all mildly embarrassing.

By the Dad GIFs editorial team · Updated November 2, 2026

Food dad jokes

Food jokes are dad-joke gold because nearly every food has a pun hiding in it. Best deployed while someone is mid-bite.

  • Why did the cookie go to the doctor?It was feeling crummy.
  • What do you call a fake noodle?An impasta.
  • Why did the tomato blush?It saw the salad dressing.
  • I’m on a seafood diet.I see food, and I eat it.
  • What do you call cheese that isn’t yours?Nacho cheese.
  • Why don’t eggs tell jokes?They’d crack each other up.
  • How do you fix a cracked pumpkin?With a pumpkin patch.
  • What did the grape do when it got stepped on?Let out a little wine.
  • Why did the banana go to the doctor?It wasn’t peeling well.
  • What do you call a sad cup of coffee?Depresso.
  • Did you hear about the restaurant on the moon?Great food, no atmosphere.

Animal dad jokes

Animal puns work on all ages, which makes them the safest choice when you don’t know your audience.

  • What do you call a bear with no teeth?A gummy bear.
  • Why don’t seagulls fly over the bay?Because then they’d be bagels.
  • What do you call a fish wearing a bowtie?Sofishticated.
  • What do you call a sleeping bull?A bulldozer.
  • Why do cows wear bells?Because their horns don’t work.
  • What do you call a pile of cats?A meowtain.
  • What do you call a dog that does magic tricks?A labracadabrador.
  • Why are frogs so happy?They eat whatever bugs them.
  • How do you count cows?With a cowculator.
  • What do you call an alligator in a vest?An investigator.

Science and math dad jokes

For the dad who has explained gravity at least once during dinner.

  • I’m reading a book about anti-gravity.It’s impossible to put down.
  • Why can’t you trust atoms?They make up everything.
  • Parallel lines have so much in common.It’s a shame they’ll never meet.
  • Why was the math book sad?It had too many problems.
  • What did the zero say to the eight?Nice belt.
  • I told a chemistry joke once.There was no reaction.
  • Why are obtuse angles so frustrated?They’re never right.
  • How does the moon cut his hair?Eclipse it.
  • What do you call an educated tube?A graduated cylinder.
  • I was going to tell a time-travel joke…but you didn’t like it.

Work and job dad jokes

Ideal for video calls, retirement cards and the office kitchen.

  • I used to be a banker,but I lost interest.
  • Why did the scarecrow win an award?He was outstanding in his field.
  • I quit my job at the shoe recycling factory.It was sole-destroying.
  • Want to hear a construction joke?Sorry, I’m still working on it.
  • I got a job at a bakery because I kneaded dough.Rye not?
  • My boss told me to have a good day,so I went home.
  • Why did the coffee file a police report?It got mugged.
  • I’d tell you a joke about paper,but it’s tearable.

How to tell a dad joke properly

A dad joke lands best when delivered with total confidence and zero shame. The groan is the goal, so lean into it.

  1. Commit to the setup. Ask the question as if it’s a serious inquiry.
  2. Pause. One beat of silence makes the punchline land harder.
  3. Deliver deadpan, then grin as the groan arrives.
  4. Never apologize. Pride is part of the genre.
  5. Know when to stop. Three in a row is a bit. Ten in a row is a hostage situation.

Quick answers

What makes something a dad joke?

A dad joke is a short, wholesome joke built on a pun or obvious wordplay, usually delivered proudly. Its humor comes as much from the groan it causes as from the joke itself.

What is the most famous dad joke?

Many people point to “Hi Hungry, I’m Dad,” said in reply to someone announcing “I’m hungry.” The scarecrow who was “outstanding in his field” is another all-time classic.
