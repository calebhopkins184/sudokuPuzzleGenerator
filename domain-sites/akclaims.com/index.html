Alaska Insurance Claims Guide: Home, Auto, Earthquake and Winter Damage

Plain-language, educational guides to filing and documenting insurance claims in Alaska, from frozen pipes and ice dams to moose collisions, earthquakes and disputes with insurers.

An insurance claim is easier when you know what to expect. AK Claims explains how homeowners and auto claims typically work, the Alaska-specific risks that cause many claims, how to document a loss, and where to turn if you disagree with your insurer, including the Alaska Division of Insurance. Your first five steps after any loss

  1. Get safe and call emergency services if needed.
  2. Stop further damage, for example by shutting off water or tarping a roof, and keep receipts.
  3. Document everything before cleanup. Here's how.
  4. Notify your insurer promptly and write down your claim number.
  5. Get a full copy of your policy and read the conditions and exclusions that apply.

Frequently asked questions

Who regulates insurance companies in Alaska?

The Alaska Division of Insurance, part of the Department of Commerce, Community, and Economic Development, licenses insurers and insurance professionals and accepts consumer complaints.

Does homeowners insurance cover earthquakes in Alaska?

Standard homeowners policies generally exclude earthquakes. Coverage requires an earthquake endorsement or separate policy, often with a percentage deductible. Learn more.

Are frozen pipes covered by insurance?

Often yes for sudden bursts in a heated, occupied home. Many policies exclude freeze damage if heat wasn't maintained or the water wasn't shut off and drained in a vacant home.

Is hitting a moose covered by car insurance?

Typically under comprehensive coverage, subject to your deductible. Liability-only coverage won't pay for your own vehicle.

What should I do if my claim is denied?

Get the decision in writing with the policy language cited, gather evidence, request reconsideration, and consider contacting the Alaska Division of Insurance or a licensed attorney. See your options.

Is AK Claims an insurance company or law firm?

No. AK Claims is an independent educational website. We don't sell insurance, adjust claims or give legal advice.
