About AK Claims

AK Claims is an independent educational website that explains how insurance claims work for Alaskans.

What we do

We write plain-language guides to homeowners and auto claims, Alaska-specific risks such as earthquakes, ice dams and frozen pipes, documenting losses, and resolving disagreements with insurers. Our goal is to help readers ask better questions and know where to find authoritative help.

What we are not

  • We are not an insurance company, agency, adjuster, public adjuster or law firm.
  • We don't sell insurance, handle claims or give legal, financial or insurance advice.
  • We are not affiliated with the Alaska Division of Insurance or any government agency.
  • We don't accept payment to refer readers to adjusters, contractors or attorneys.

Important disclaimer

AK Claims provides general educational information only. It is not legal, financial or insurance advice, and AK Claims is not an insurance company, adjuster or law firm. Your policy language controls. For advice about your situation, consult a licensed Alaska attorney, a licensed adjuster or the Alaska Division of Insurance.

Laws, regulations and policy forms change. Always read your own policy and verify current requirements with official sources such as the Alaska Division of Insurance.
