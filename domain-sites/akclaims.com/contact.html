Contact AK Claims

The best way to reach us is by email: hello@akclaims.com.

We welcome corrections and ideas for new guides. We can't review policies, advise on individual claims or recommend specific adjusters or attorneys. For help with a claim, contact your insurer, the Alaska Division of Insurance, or a licensed professional.

Please don't send policy numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive personal information by email.
