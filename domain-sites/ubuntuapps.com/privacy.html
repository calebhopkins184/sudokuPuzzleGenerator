Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026. This policy explains what information UbuntuApps (ubuntuapps.com) collects and how it is used. Short version: very little, and we don't sell it.

What we collect

UbuntuApps is a static website of articles. There are no user accounts, no comments, and no checkout.

  • Server logs. Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. These logs are used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Analytics. We may use privacy-respecting analytics that count page views in aggregate without building advertising profiles of individual visitors.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply.

Cookies

The site itself does not need cookies to work and does not set advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this page.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • We do not share your email address with marketers.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to outside websites, including government agencies and software projects. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any email correspondence we hold from you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access or delete personal information; write to us and we will honor applicable requests.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@ubuntuapps.com.
