Contact UbuntuApps

Spotted an outdated command or want to suggest an app? Email us, and include your Ubuntu version (run lsb_release -a) if you're reporting a problem.

Email

The best way to reach us is email: hello@ubuntuapps.com. We read every message, but we are a small editorial site, so replies can take a few days.

Send a message

This form opens your own email program with your message filled in. Nothing is stored on our server.

Before you write

  • Corrections are welcome. If something in a guide is wrong or out of date, tell us which page and what changed. Include a source link if you have one.
  • We cannot give one-on-one support. We write general guides and are not able to troubleshoot individual accounts, devices, or purchases.
  • Please don't send sensitive data. Never email passwords, account numbers, or government ID numbers to us (or to anyone who asks for them out of the blue).
