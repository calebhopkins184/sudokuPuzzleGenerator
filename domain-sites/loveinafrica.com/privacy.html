Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains what information Love in Africa (loveinafrica.com) collects and how it is used. The short version: we collect very little, and we don’t sell anything about you.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Information we collect

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, no comment system and no shopping cart, so there is nothing to sign up for and no profile stored about you.

  • Server logs. Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Analytics. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are useful. If we do, we choose tools that do not build advertising profiles or track you across other websites.
  • Email. If you email us or use the contact form (which simply opens your own email app), we receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message.

How we use information

We use the information above only to operate and improve the site and to reply to messages. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Cookies

The site does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics or other features that use cookies in the future, we will update this page.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to outside sources, such as government travel advisories and official registries. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Children’s privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and for ordinary record keeping. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@loveinafrica.com.
