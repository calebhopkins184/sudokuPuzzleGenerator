About Love in Africa

Love in Africa is an independent website for couples planning honeymoons, destination weddings, anniversaries and romantic journeys across Africa.

What we cover

  • Honeymoon destinations, from island beaches to safari camps
  • Planning advice for destination weddings, including legal vs symbolic ceremonies
  • Respectful introductions to wedding traditions from specific African cultures
  • Sample itineraries built around realistic travel times

What we are not

Love in Africa is not a dating or matchmaking site, a travel agency or a wedding planner. We don’t take bookings, and we don’t publish reviews, testimonials or ratings.

Our standards

  • Specific, not stereotyped. Africa is a continent of 54 countries; we name the place and the culture.
  • Honest about trade-offs. Weather, tides, travel time and cost all matter.
  • Official sources for rules. Entry, health and marriage requirements change, so we direct you to governments and registries rather than repeating details that may go out of date.
  • Inclusive. We include practical safety information for LGBTQ+ couples.

Corrections

If you notice something inaccurate — including how a tradition is described — please tell us. We welcome input from people who know these cultures firsthand.
