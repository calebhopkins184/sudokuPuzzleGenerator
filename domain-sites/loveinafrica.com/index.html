Frequently asked questions

Where should we go in Africa for our honeymoon?

For beaches, consider the Seychelles, Mauritius or Zanzibar. For food and wine, South Africa’s Cape Winelands. For adventure, a safari in Tanzania, Kenya, Botswana or South Africa, ideally followed by a few days on a beach.

What is the best time of year for an African honeymoon?

It depends on the destination. June to October suits most safaris and Zanzibar; April to May and October to November suit the Seychelles; May to December suits Mauritius; November to March suits Cape Town and the Winelands.

Can foreigners get legally married in Africa?

Yes, in several countries. South Africa, Mauritius and the Seychelles are established options for visitors, each with document and notice requirements. Many couples marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony in Africa.

Is Africa safe for honeymooners?

Popular honeymoon areas are visited by many couples each year. Use reputable operators, follow local guidance and check your government’s travel advisory for each country, as conditions vary widely across the continent.

Is Love in Africa a dating site?

No. Love in Africa is a travel and culture guide for couples planning honeymoons, weddings and romantic trips. We don’t offer dating or matchmaking services.

How long should an African honeymoon be?

Ten to fourteen days works well for most couples, allowing for long-haul flights, a safari of three to five nights and time to relax on a beach.
