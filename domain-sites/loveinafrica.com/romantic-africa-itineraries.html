Romantic Africa Itineraries: Six Sample Trips for Couples

A great romantic itinerary in Africa has fewer stops than you expect. Six sample routes follow, each designed around two or three bases, realistic travel times and a restful finish. Use them as starting shapes to adapt with a reputable local operator, adjusting for season and budget.

By the Love in Africa editorial team · Updated November 2, 2026

How these itineraries are built

Each route keeps at least three nights per stop where possible, puts the most relaxing part last, and avoids long overland days after international flights. Night counts are approximate; transfer times depend on flights and road conditions.

1. Tanzania safari and Zanzibar (12 nights)

Best for: classic bush-and-beach honeymoons. Best months: June–October or January–February.

  • Nights 1–2: Arusha area. Recover from the flight at a coffee-farm lodge.
  • Nights 3–4: Tarangire or Ngorongoro highlands. Elephants and baobabs, then a day on the Ngorongoro Crater floor.
  • Nights 5–7: Serengeti. Choose the area by season using a migration-savvy operator; consider a balloon safari.
  • Nights 8–12: Zanzibar. One night in Stone Town, then four on the north or east coast. See the Zanzibar guide.

2. South Africa: Cape Town, Winelands and Kruger (13 nights)

Best for: food lovers, first-time Africa visitors and couples who want variety. Best months: October–April for the Cape; Kruger is good year-round.

  • Nights 1–4: Cape Town. Table Mountain, Cape Point, Chapman’s Peak Drive and the penguins at Boulders Beach.
  • Nights 5–7: Franschhoek or Stellenbosch. Wine tastings, long lunches and mountain walks.
  • Nights 8–10: Private reserve bordering Kruger. Small camp, off-road tracking and bush dinners.
  • Nights 11–13: Optional add-on. Fly to Mauritius or Mozambique’s coast, or the Garden Route by road.

3. Seychelles island hopping (9 nights)

Best for: beach purists. Best months: April–May or October–November.

  • Nights 1–3: Mahé. Beaches such as Anse Intendance, mountain trails in Morne Seychellois National Park, and Victoria’s market.
  • Nights 4–6: Praslin. The Vallée de Mai’s coco de mer palms and Anse Lazio.
  • Nights 7–9: La Digue. Cycle to Anse Source d’Argent and Grand Anse; quiet evenings.

Inter-island ferries and short flights connect the main islands. Check timetables, as sea conditions affect crossings.

4. Botswana, Victoria Falls and Cape Town (12 nights)

Best for: luxury wilderness and big-ticket experiences. Best months: June–October.

  • Nights 1–3: Okavango Delta camp. Mokoro trips and game drives; fly-in by light aircraft.
  • Nights 4–6: A second Delta or Linyanti/Chobe camp. Different habitat, different wildlife.
  • Nights 7–8: Victoria Falls. Guided tour of the falls, a Zambezi sunset cruise, and Devil’s Pool if it is open.
  • Nights 9–12: Cape Town. Finish with city restaurants and coastal drives.

Light-aircraft luggage limits apply; pack soft bags. Our safari honeymoon guide has more tips.

5. Morocco: medina, mountains and desert (9 nights)

Best for: culture, design and adventure. Best months: March–May or September–November.

  • Nights 1–3: Marrakech. A riad in the medina, hammam, gardens and rooftop dinners.
  • Night 4: Atlas Mountains. A mountain lodge and a gentle hike to Berber villages.
  • Nights 5–6: Sahara. Travel via the Dadès or Todra gorges to a desert camp in the Erg Chebbi dunes; sunrise on the sand.
  • Nights 7–9: Essaouira or back to Marrakech. Sea breezes, seafood and slower days.

Drives to the Sahara are long, so a private driver makes them more comfortable.

6. Mauritius slow honeymoon (10 nights)

Best for: couples who want to unpack once. Best months: May–December.

  • Nights 1–5: West or southwest coast. Sunsets, Le Morne Brabant and a trip to Chamarel and Black River Gorges.
  • Nights 6–10: East coast. Calm lagoons, a boat trip to Île aux Cerfs and spa days.

Splitting between two coasts gives variety without much travel time, since the island is compact.

Choosing between these routes

To unpack once and truly restMauritius

Shorter on time? Most routes compress well by dropping one base. A week in the Seychelles with two islands, or five nights on safari plus four in Zanzibar, still feels unhurried. Longer trips can add a second safari region or a few nights in a city, but resist adding more than one extra stop.

Tips for adapting any itinerary

  1. Check seasons first using the destination notes in our honeymoon destinations guide.
  2. Allow a buffer night before international departures, especially after light-aircraft flights.
  3. Confirm entry rules for every country, including visas, passport blank pages and yellow fever certificates.
  4. Include rest days. The best honeymoon memories rarely come from the busiest days.
