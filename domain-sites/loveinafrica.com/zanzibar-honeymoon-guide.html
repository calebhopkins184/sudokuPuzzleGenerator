Zanzibar Honeymoon Guide: Beaches, Stone Town and Spice

Zanzibar is one of Africa’s most popular honeymoon islands because it combines warm Indian Ocean beaches, a historic old town and easy access from Tanzania’s safari parks. The key decision is which coast to stay on: the north for swimming at any tide and sunsets, the east for wide tidal beaches and seclusion.

By the Love in Africa editorial team · Updated November 2, 2026

Which coast should you choose?

Your choice of coast shapes your honeymoon more than any hotel feature, mainly because of the tides. Zanzibar’s east coast has a large tidal range: at low tide the sea can retreat hundreds of meters, leaving sandbanks, seaweed farms and rock pools. It is beautiful, but you may not be able to swim for hours at a time. The north coast generally has deeper water close to shore.

Many couples split their stay: a night or two in Stone Town, then four or five nights on the beach.

Stone Town

Stone Town, the old quarter of Zanzibar City, was listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2000 for its blend of Swahili, Arab, Persian, Indian and European influences. Spend a day getting pleasantly lost among its alleys and carved wooden doors, then:

  • Take a guided heritage walk, including the site of the former slave market and its memorial — a sobering, important part of the island’s history.
  • Watch the sunset from a rooftop terrace, then browse the evening food stalls at Forodhani Gardens on the waterfront.
  • Visit the market and spice stalls, and look for the house associated with Freddie Mercury, who was born in Zanzibar.

Romantic things to do

  • Sunset dhow cruise: sail on a traditional wooden boat as the sun drops into the sea off the west or north coast.
  • Snorkeling at Mnemba Atoll: a marine conservation area off the north-east coast, with clear water and reef fish. Choose operators that respect rules about not chasing or crowding dolphins.
  • Spice farm tour: Zanzibar has long been known as a “Spice Island” and is a major clove producer. Tours show cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, vanilla and pepper growing.
  • Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park: walk through forest to see the Zanzibar red colobus, a monkey found only here, plus a mangrove boardwalk.
  • Prison (Changuu) Island: a short boat ride from Stone Town to see giant tortoises and snorkel.
  • Sandbank picnics: boat trips to sandbanks that appear at low tide, with seafood lunches.

When to go

The best months for a Zanzibar honeymoon are June to October (dry, slightly cooler and breezy) and December to February (hot and mostly dry). The long rains, roughly March to May, bring heavy showers, and some hotels close for maintenance. November’s short rains tend to be lighter and briefer. Being near the equator, Zanzibar is warm all year.

Culture and etiquette

Zanzibar is predominantly Muslim and socially conservative. Respecting local norms makes your trip smoother and is simply good manners.

  • Dress: beachwear is fine at resorts and on beaches. In Stone Town and villages, both partners should cover shoulders and knees.
  • Affection: keep public displays of affection modest away from your hotel.
  • Ramadan: during the holy month, avoid eating, drinking or smoking in public in daylight outside tourist areas; some restaurants close or change hours.
  • Photography: ask before photographing people.
  • Greetings: a few words of Swahili go a long way — jambo (hello), asante (thank you), karibu (welcome).

LGBTQ+ couples: same-sex relations are criminalized in Tanzania, including Zanzibar. Review your government’s travel advisory carefully. South Africa, which recognizes same-sex marriage, may be a more welcoming choice; see our destination weddings guide.

Practical tips

  • Entry: Zanzibar is part of Tanzania, so Tanzanian visa rules apply; many nationalities use the official e-visa system. Zanzibar has also introduced a mandatory travel insurance requirement for foreign visitors. Check current requirements before you book.
  • Health: ask a travel clinic about malaria prevention and whether a yellow fever certificate is needed for your route.
  • Getting there: fly into Zanzibar’s international airport directly or connect via Dar es Salaam, Arusha or Kilimanjaro; there are also ferries from Dar es Salaam.
  • Plastic bags: Tanzania bans single-use plastic bags, so pack without them.
  • Tell your hotel you’re on honeymoon — many arrange small touches, though never assume upgrades.

Quick answers

Is Zanzibar good for a honeymoon?

Yes. Zanzibar combines warm beaches, the historic Stone Town and easy access from Tanzania’s safari parks, which makes it one of Africa’s most popular honeymoon islands.

Which part of Zanzibar is best for honeymooners?

Choose the north (Nungwi or Kendwa) for swimming at most tides and sunsets, or the east and Michamvi for quieter, more secluded beaches with large tides.
