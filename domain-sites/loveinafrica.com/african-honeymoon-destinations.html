The Best African Honeymoon Destinations

The best African honeymoon destination depends on what “romantic” means to you. For powder-white beaches, choose the Seychelles, Mauritius or Zanzibar. For food, wine and scenery, the Cape Winelands. For wildlife and adventure, a safari, ideally paired with a beach. Below, eight standout choices with honest trade-offs and the best months to go.

By the Love in Africa editorial team · Updated November 2, 2026

1. Zanzibar

Zanzibar is ideal for couples who want beaches with culture. The island mixes palm-lined coast with Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site of carved doors, spice markets and rooftop sunsets. It is a short flight from Tanzania’s safari parks, making it the classic “bush and beach” finale. Read our full Zanzibar honeymoon guide.

2. Seychelles

The Seychelles is the choice for postcard beaches and privacy. This archipelago of 115 islands in the Indian Ocean is known for granite boulders framing turquoise water. Base yourself on Mahé, then take the ferry to Praslin, home of the UNESCO-listed Vallée de Mai and its coco de mer palms, and to La Digue, where bicycles are the main way to get around and Anse Source d’Argent is one of the world’s most photographed beaches. The transition months between the trade winds, roughly April–May and October–November, usually bring the calmest seas.

3. Mauritius

Mauritius suits couples who want polished resorts plus things to do. A coral reef surrounds much of the island, creating calm lagoons for snorkeling and paddleboarding. Inland, there are the Black River Gorges, the Chamarel Seven Coloured Earth and waterfalls; on the southwest coast, the dramatic Le Morne Brabant mountain is a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape. The cooler, drier months from May to December are most comfortable; cyclones are possible roughly from November to April.

4. Cape Town and the Cape Winelands

South Africa’s Western Cape is perfect for food-and-wine couples. Spend days in Cape Town (Table Mountain, Chapman’s Peak Drive, Boulders Beach penguins), then move to the Winelands: Stellenbosch, Franschhoek and Paarl, with world-class restaurants and tastings in mountain valleys. The region has a Mediterranean climate — warm, dry summers from about November to March and wet winters. If you travel from roughly July to November, add Hermanus for land-based whale watching.

5. Victoria Falls

Victoria Falls, known locally as Mosi-oa-Tunya (“the smoke that thunders”), is on the Zambia–Zimbabwe border. The flow peaks roughly from March to May, when the spray is immense. In the lower-water months (roughly mid-August to January, depending on the year), the famous Devil’s Pool on the Zambian side may open for guided swims at the lip of the falls. Sunset cruises on the Zambezi River are a romantic classic. It pairs easily with Botswana’s Chobe or a Zambian safari.

6. Morocco

Morocco offers romance of a different kind: candlelit riads in Marrakech’s medina, mountain lodges in the Atlas, a night in a desert camp among the Erg Chebbi dunes near Merzouga, and the breezy coastal town of Essaouira. Spring and autumn are most comfortable; desert nights can be cold in winter. Morocco is conservative about public displays of affection, so keep romance discreet in public.

7. Mozambique

Mozambique’s islands are for couples who want remoteness. The Bazaruto Archipelago and the Quirimbas offer dhow sailing, diving and some of the last dugong habitat in the western Indian Ocean. Travel takes more effort, and you should check current government travel advisories, particularly for the far north.

8. Namibia

Namibia rewards couples who love space and silence: the red dunes of Sossusvlei, the Skeleton Coast and some of the darkest night skies on Earth. The NamibRand Nature Reserve is an International Dark Sky Reserve. Many couples road-trip in a 4x4 between lodges and finish at Etosha for wildlife.

How to choose

  • Match the season first. A destination at its best beats a “better” one in the wrong month.
  • Limit flights. Two destinations in ten days is relaxing; four is exhausting. See our romantic itineraries.
  • Combine wildlife and water. Our safari honeymoon guide explains bush-and-beach pairings.
  • Check entry and health rules for every country, including visas, yellow fever certificates and malaria advice.

Quick answers

What is the most romantic place in Africa for a honeymoon?

There is no single answer, but the Seychelles is often considered the most romantic beach destination, while a private safari camp followed by Zanzibar or Mauritius is the classic African honeymoon combination.

Is Mauritius or Seychelles better for a honeymoon?

Mauritius is better for resort variety, activities and value; the Seychelles is better for wild, boulder-framed beaches and island hopping between quieter islands.
