About TaxPro Florida

TaxPro Florida is an independent educational website that explains Florida taxes in plain English. Despite the name, we are not a tax preparation firm, CPA firm or law firm. We don't prepare returns, represent taxpayers or give individual advice. Our goal is to help Floridians understand the system well enough to ask the right questions and to find qualified help when they need it.

What we cover

  • Florida's lack of a state income tax and how residency works
  • The homestead exemption and Save Our Homes
  • Florida sales and use tax
  • Business taxes: corporate income, reemployment and more
  • Choosing a CPA, enrolled agent or tax attorney

Editorial standards

  • We base our guides on Florida statutes and official guidance from the Florida Department of Revenue, county property appraisers and the IRS.
  • Where rates or rules change often, such as county surtaxes or inflation-adjusted exemptions, we point you to the official source rather than quoting a number that could go stale.
  • Examples with dollar amounts are illustrative only.
  • We don't accept payment to recommend specific professionals or firms.

Corrections

Tax law changes regularly. If you spot something outdated, please let us know.
