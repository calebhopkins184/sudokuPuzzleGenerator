Florida has no state personal income tax, but that's only part of the picture. Personal income taxNone; prohibited by the Florida Constitution
Sales tax6% state rate plus a county surtax in most counties

Official sources we rely on

  • Florida Department of Revenue: sales and use tax, corporate income tax, reemployment tax and property tax oversight

  • Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz): business registration and annual reports
  • IRS: federal income tax, which still applies to Floridians

Frequently asked questions

Does Florida have a state income tax?

No. Florida doesn't tax individuals' income, including wages, retirement income and investment income. You still file a federal return with the IRS.

What is the homestead exemption deadline in Florida?

Generally March 1. You must own the home and make it your permanent residence by January 1 of that tax year, then apply with your county property appraiser.

What is Florida's sales tax rate?

6% statewide, plus a discretionary county surtax in most counties. The surtax generally applies only to the first $5,000 of a single item's price.

Do LLCs pay Florida corporate income tax?

Only if the LLC is taxed as a corporation for federal purposes. LLCs taxed as partnerships or disregarded entities generally don't, though a corporate owner may have to file.

Is TaxPro Florida a tax preparation firm?

No. We're an independent educational website. We don't prepare returns or give individual advice. For that, consult a CPA, enrolled agent or tax attorney.

How do I check if a Florida CPA is licensed?

Use the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation's online license search. For enrolled agents and other credentialed preparers, use the IRS directory of preparers.
