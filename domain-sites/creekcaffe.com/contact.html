Contact Creek Caffe

The quickest way to reach Creek Caffe is by email. We read every message, and we especially welcome corrections — if something on this site is out of date or wrong, please tell us.

Email: hello@creekcaffe.com

Brewing question? Include your method, recipe (coffee and water amounts), grind and what the coffee tastes like — it helps us help you.

Send a message

What we can and can’t help with

  • We can answer general brewing questions and fix errors in our guides.
  • We can’t review products for payment, sell equipment or troubleshoot specific machine faults — contact your manufacturer for repairs.
