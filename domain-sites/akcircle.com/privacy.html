Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information AK Circle (akcircle.com) collects when you visit and how it is used. The short version: this is a static informational website. We don't sell personal data, we don't run user accounts, and we keep data collection to a minimum.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us or use the contact form (which opens your own email app), we receive whatever you include, such as your name, email address and message. We use it only to read and reply to you.
  • Basic server logs. Like nearly every website host, our hosting provider may automatically record standard request data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit. These logs are used for security, troubleshooting and keeping the site running.
  • Analytics. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are useful, in aggregate. We do not use analytics to build advertising profiles of individual visitors.

Cookies

This site does not require cookies to work and does not set advertising cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this page.

How we use information

We use information only to operate and improve the website and to respond to messages. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the site (such as web hosting or email), when required by law, or to protect the security of the site and its visitors.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to government agencies and other outside resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and your choices

We keep emails only as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete correspondence you've sent by emailing hello@akcircle.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@akcircle.com.
