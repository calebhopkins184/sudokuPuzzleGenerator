About StockQB

StockQB — short for "stock quarterback" — is an independent investing education site. A quarterback reads the field, knows the playbook, and makes calm decisions under pressure. That is what we want to help beginning investors do.

What you will find here

  • Plain-language explainers on stock quotes, valuation, fund types, dividends, order types, and portfolio construction.
  • Worked examples using hypothetical tickers and round numbers, so you can follow the math.
  • Links to primary sources such as Investor.gov (the SEC's investor education site), FINRA, and IRS.gov for rules that change.

What you will not find here

  • Stock picks, price targets, or "hot tips."
  • Paid promotions disguised as education.
  • Personalized investment advice.

StockQB is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, or financial planner. Before making investment decisions, consider your own goals and circumstances and consult a licensed professional if you need personal advice. You can check the background of a financial professional using FINRA's free BrokerCheck tool.

Spot an error?

Markets and rules change. If something on the site is out of date or unclear, please let us know.
