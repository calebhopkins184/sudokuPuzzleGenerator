Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

This privacy policy explains what information StockQB ("we", "us") collects when you visit stockqb.com and how it is used. The short version: this is a static informational website. We do not require accounts, we do not sell personal information, and we collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us at hello@stockqb.com or use the contact form (which simply opens your own email app), we receive your email address, name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the site.
  • Basic server logs. Like nearly every website, the server or hosting provider that delivers these pages may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, referring page, and time of request. These logs are used for security, troubleshooting, and keeping the site running.
  • Analytics. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are useful, in aggregate. If we do, we prefer tools that do not use cross-site tracking cookies or build advertising profiles.

Cookies

This site does not need cookies to work, and we do not set advertising cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this page.

How we use information

We use the limited information above to respond to messages, fix problems, protect the site from abuse, and decide what content to improve. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help run the site (such as hosting or email), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of the site and its visitors.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to outside sources such as government agencies and official help pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children

This site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any email correspondence you have sent by writing to hello@stockqb.com. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@stockqb.com.
