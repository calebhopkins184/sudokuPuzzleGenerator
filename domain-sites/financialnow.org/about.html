About FinancialNow

FinancialNow is an independent educational website about personal finance basics. Our goal is simple: explain the core money concepts that schools often skip — budgeting, emergency savings, credit, debt payoff, index funds, and retirement accounts — clearly enough that you can make your own informed decisions.

What we believe

  • Basics beat tricks. Spending less than you earn, avoiding high-interest debt, and investing steadily in low-cost diversified funds do most of the work.
  • Show the math. When we compare options, we use worked examples so you can see why one approach costs more or less.
  • Point to the source. Rules that change, such as contribution limits and tax thresholds, should be confirmed with official sources like IRS.gov, the CFPB, and the FDIC.

What we are not

FinancialNow is not a bank, broker, investment adviser, tax preparer, or law firm, and we do not offer personalized advice. We do not sell financial products. Our guides are general education; your situation may call for a licensed professional such as a Certified Financial Planner, CPA, or attorney.

Corrections

We try hard to be accurate and to keep pages current. If you spot an error or an outdated rule, please contact us and we will review it.
