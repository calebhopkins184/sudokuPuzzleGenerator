About High Qulity

High Qulity exists to help people buy fewer, better things. And yes, we know.

About the name

We spend our budget on quality, not spelling. The name is a small joke with a real point: labels, logos, and marketing copy don't tell you much about how something is made. A brand can spell "quality" perfectly on the box and still glue the soles on. We'd rather teach you to check the stitching.

What we cover

Our guides explain the construction signals that separate durable products from disposable ones in clothing, cookware, hand tools, furniture, and footwear, plus how to weigh price against lifespan using cost per use.

How we work

  • Construction over brands. Companies change factories, owners, and materials. The signs of good construction don't change, so that's what we teach.
  • No products, no affiliate links. We don't sell anything and we don't get paid when you buy something.
  • No fake reviews or test scores. We describe how things are made and what to look for. We don't publish invented ratings or lab results.
  • Corrections welcome. If you know a trade better than we do (tailors, cobblers, woodworkers, we're looking at you), tell us what we got wrong.
