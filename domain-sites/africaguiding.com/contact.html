Contact Africa Guiding

The quickest way to reach Africa Guiding is by email. We read every message, and we especially welcome corrections — if something on this site is out of date or wrong, please tell us.

What we can and can’t help with

  • We can answer general questions about our guides and fix errors.
  • We can’t book safaris, sell permits, recommend specific paid operators, or give medical or visa advice. For those, contact licensed operators, official wildlife authorities, your embassy and a travel clinician.
