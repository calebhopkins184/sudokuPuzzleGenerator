About Finance North

Finance North is an independent educational website about personal finance for people living in Alaska and other northern places. National money advice often ignores the realities of life up here: seasonal paychecks, grocery prices far above national averages in many towns, heating bills that triple in January, and flights that cost more than a week's pay. We write for those realities.

What we cover

  • The Permanent Fund Dividend: eligibility, taxes and smart uses
  • Budgeting for Alaska's cost of living
  • Comparing and cutting heating costs
  • How Alaska's tax system works
  • Saving for travel Outside and around the state

Editorial standards

  • We rely on official sources such as the Alaska Department of Revenue, the IRS, the Alaska Housing Finance Corporation and local governments.
  • We avoid stating figures that change often, such as the current PFD amount or local tax rates, and instead tell you where to find the current number.
  • Any example numbers are clearly labeled as hypothetical.
  • We don't accept payment for recommendations and don't sell financial products.

What we are not

Finance North isn't a bank, financial advisor, tax preparer or government agency, and our content isn't personalized advice. Please consult a licensed professional before making significant financial decisions.
